What the agent can do

Connected to the Wellytics scheduling system, patient database and clinical triage engine.

Patient lookup

Finds returning patients by phone number or name and verifies with date of birth.

Symptom triage

Asks focused follow-up questions and recommends the right department using your history.

Appointment booking

Checks open slots, confirms date and AM/PM time with you, then books instantly.

Multilingual

Starts in English and switches to the patient's language: Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Tamil, French, Spanish, German, Russian, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Ukrainian or Dutch.

How a call works

A typical conversation takes just a few minutes from hello to confirmed booking.

Identify

Speak in your language and share your registered mobile number.

Select hospital

Pick the hospital or branch you'd like to visit.

Describe symptoms

Answer a couple of quick questions so we route you to the right specialist.

Confirm booking

Agree on a date and time — your appointment is booked on the call.

Before you start

A few tips for the best experience.

  • Allow microphone access when your browser asks.
  • Find a quiet spot and speak at a normal pace.
  • Keep your registered mobile number handy.
  • In a medical emergency, call 108 instead.
